My Favourite Character!

Arthur Morgan

_______________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________________
  • About

    Arthur morgan is our protagonist of Red Dead Redemption II set in the year 1899. Arthur had spent most of his life with an outlaw gang that he had been loyal to since the day he joined with Dutch van der Linde and Hosea Matthews taking him in when he was a troubled 14 year old. Through the many hours I have played in this game Arthur's Morgan's character is well written with many hours of dialogue based on decisions the player makes, from cutscenes to interactions with NPC's being different depending on the honour system in the game, especially when Arthur contracts tuberculosis where we see his views of the gang changing.

  • Personality & Habits

    • Arthur's personality is a no-nonsense, sarcastic and stoic type in the beginning of the game anyway. A couple of habits that Arthur has is say something sarcastic or smartass when something goes wrong, put himself in danger to save the gang members, journaling points of interest and drawing them. If players choose the high honour path, we unlock special high honour side missions, showing Arthur's personality become more compassionate towards others, open about his feelings and finally see Dutch in a different light after many events that led Dutch to go crazy. One particular mission where we see Arthur openly show emotion, was when he talks to Sister Calderon, a catholic nun that Arthur speaks to before she heads on the train to Mexico for a religious mission. They both sit down on a bench where Arthur starts chatting to her and eventually Arthur confesses he is dying of tuberculosis after contracting it from beating a man for a couple of bucks and that he is afraid after living a bad life. Sister Calderon told him "there is nothing to be afraid of and to take a gamble that love exists and do a loving act."

  • Knowledge and Skills

    • Arthur has many skills ranging from combat, sharpshooting and artistic skills with the gang members in the game often talk about Arthur's greatest strengths. During chapter 2, Arthur and a couple of gang members, Javier Escuella, Bill Williamson and Charles Smith go out to drink in the saloon in town called Valentine where Bill had started a bar fight, leading to an NPC named Tommy arriving the scene to join the fight. Arthur and Tommy begin brawling with him being thrown out the window where the two had continued their fight outside on the muddy ground, at first it was tough but then Arthur gets the advantage, beating Tommy over and over again until a bystander pulls Arthur back, telling Arthur he had already won the fight and not to beat him further. After this mission, there is a chance you can run into Tommy in the very same saloon where an NPC states that Tommy has not been the same since as he had been brain damaged from the fight. Arthur's art skills are loved by many players, from his drawings of buildings, animals, plants and people. The journal in the game was so loved by players that there is actual journal replicas for people to purchase which is fun for fans.

  • Associates

    • Arthur pretty much grew up with the gang, seeing some coming and going, keeping a good relationship with a few especially during the final chapters. On the very first chapter, we meet Sadie Adler, recently widowed as their rival gang the O'Driscolls had killed her husband Jake and took over their home to party and warm up from the cold mountains. Arthur, Dutch and Micah Bell kill off the O'driscolls from the house where Arthur and Dutch had brought Sadie to the safety of their camp since Micah had set the house on fire. Sadie Adler's character builds up and during chapter 3, she wanted to be the one shooting with Arthur and the others simply to kill off every O'driscoll out of grief and vengeance. Arthur and Sadie have a close friendship with Sadie only trusting Arthur and Charles out of the whole gang, sometimes asking Arthur and her to go on solo missions to kill more O'Driscolls or saving gang members. Charles Smith is another close friend of Arthur's and during a chapter 2 mission, he and Charles go out hunting for a bison with much success and discovering something horrifying. The two find many bison killed and left to rot with nothing being harvested. This sparked Charles' anger so the two track down culprits in a camp where Charles had shot one and got the other to speak, finding out that the guys have been paid to make it look like the Native Americans did it, the player has an option to shoot the poacher or not. Rains Fall is the chief of the Wapiti reservation that help build Arthur's character, and if you are at a high honour, Arthur tells Rains Fall about his son and the mother of his son's death which had haunted Arthur for years. Arthur narrates after Rains Fall's mission, where his perspective shifts. "He's a man who not so long ago, I would have found weak and pathetic. Now I see as wise and thoughtful and sensible"-Arthur Morgan
